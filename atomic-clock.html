Testing Weak Frequency-Dependent Residuals with Optical Atomic Clocks

A prospective experimental framework

Author: P. Gabor (Palásti Gábor)

Publication: 27 August 2026 · Version 1

Resource type: Preprint

DOI:10.5281/zenodo.22133035

This work presents a prospective framework for testing weakfrequency-dependent residual effects using high-precision opticalatomic clocks. The study emphasizes sensitivity, experimentalcontrols, reproducibility and falsifiability. No detection ofnew physics is claimed.
