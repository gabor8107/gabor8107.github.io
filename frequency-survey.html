QRUM Astrophysical Frequency Survey

A Unified Statistical Analysis of Compact Object Frequency Distributions

Author: P. Gabor (Palásti Gábor)

Publication: 21 August 2026 · Version 2

Resource type: Book

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22050657

This work was originally developed within the broader Quantum Resonance Unified Model (QRUM) research programme. The revised version presents the astrophysical frequency survey as a statistically testable and independently reproducible investigation. QRUM is retained as a possible interpretative framework and is not assumed or claimed to be established by the statistical results presented herein.

Publicly available observational datasets of pulsating white dwarfs, pulsars, magnetars and high-frequency quasi-periodic oscillations are analysed using a common statistical framework based on Kernel Density Estimation (KDE), Gaussian Mixture Modelling (GMM), Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC) model selection and bootstrap validation.

The objective is not to claim direct confirmation of a new physical theory, but to provide a reproducible statistical reference framework for future independent investigations of compact-object frequency distributions.
