QRUM Environmental Dependence

Pulsar Frequency vs Galactic Dark Matter Density

Author: P. Gabor (Palásti Gábor)

Publication: 30 July 2026 · Version 1.0

Resource type: Preprint

DOI:10.5281/zenodo.21694558

This study investigates the relationship between pulsar characteristic frequencies and Galactic dark matter density within the broader Quantum Resonance Unified Model (QRUM) research programme.

The analysis is designed as an observational and statistically testable investigation of possible environmental dependence in compact-object frequency populations. The work emphasizes reproducibility, transparent statistical analysis and the use of publicly accessible astrophysical data.

QRUM is treated as a possible phenomenological interpretative framework rather than an established physical theory. The statistical analysis does not constitute confirmation of new physics, and independent replication and systematic examination of conventional explanations remain essential.
