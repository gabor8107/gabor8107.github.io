QRUM Environmental Dependence

Pulsar Frequency vs Galactic Dark Matter Density

Author: P. Gabor (Palásti Gábor)

Publication: 30 July 2026 · Version 1.0

Resource type: Preprint

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21694558

This study investigates the relationship between pulsar characteristic frequencies and Galactic dark matter density within the broader Quantum Resonance Unified Model (QRUM) research programme.

The analysis is presented as a reproducible statistical investigation using observational astrophysical data and an open computational workflow. Environmental correlations are examined as potentially testable patterns rather than as established evidence for a new physical mechanism.

The study is exploratory. The results do not establish QRUM or constitute a confirmed detection of new physics. Independent replication and systematic examination of conventional explanations remain essential.
